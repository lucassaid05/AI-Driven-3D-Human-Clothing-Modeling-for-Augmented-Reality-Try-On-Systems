Research notice & use of data

AI-driven 3D human clothing modelling for augmented reality try-on — MCAST dissertation research (Lucas Said)

Prototype only. This application is not a commercial product or a finished service. It exists solely to support academic research on AR try-on, pose estimation, and 3D garment alignment. Do not rely on it for purchasing decisions, medical, or safety-related purposes.

Purpose

The study evaluates an AR prototype that uses real-time pose estimation to align 3D garment assets with a person’s upper body. The research focuses on tracking stability, alignment quality, rendering performance, and usability. The work is conducted under MCAST’s research ethics framework, including Document 074 Research Ethics Policy and Procedure, as referenced in the approved Statement of Intent.

Participation and consent

Taking part in the research is voluntary. Adults (18+) may be invited after receiving a Participant Information Letter and, where applicable, completing a consent process outside this webpage. No personal data is collected through this page itself.

Research data is collected only from participants who have agreed to take part in line with that information and consent (for example, after signing a consent form or confirming equivalent approval as required by your institution). If you have not been through that process, you should use this software only in a non-research context with no expectation that your session contributes to the study—or refrain from use until you have read the participant materials and agreed appropriately.

What data the research may collect (when consented)

As described in the ethics documentation for this project, agreed participation may involve:

  • System performance information (for example FPS, latency, device model).
  • Pose-estimation outputs, such as anonymised landmark coordinates, and alignment metrics derived from them.
  • A short post-task questionnaire and optional written comments.

Audio recording is not part of the design. Visual recordings (for example screen or camera footage) are not collected unless strictly necessary and only with separate explicit consent; if used, identifiability is reduced where possible (for example avoiding or obscuring faces), and material is handled under the retention rules below.

No biometric data is collected or stored for the purpose of identifying individuals. If the methodology ever changes in that respect, additional clearance from the MCAST Data Protection Officer would be required before proceeding.

Ethical principles (summary)

Research is conducted to avoid undue psychological, physical, or financial harm. In line with the approved ethics section:

  • Access to identifiable participant-linked data is limited to the researcher and supervisor; raw participant data is not shared with examiners as described in the SOI.
  • Participants may withdraw at any time without giving a reason. On withdrawal, participant-linked records are deleted as set out in the Participant Information Letter (for example, notification within 14 days quoting a participant code). Fully anonymised aggregated data that cannot be linked back may be retained for analysis.
  • The participant remains the owner of their data; processing is for this study only, with a transparent collection process.
  • Confidentiality, anonymity, and data protection procedures apply. Identifiers such as names or ID numbers are not required; a participant code may be used. Data are stored in password-protected, access-controlled storage; consent materials are kept separately from research data.
  • Data are retained only as long as needed for the dissertation and any required follow-up, then deleted within the period stated in the participant documentation (for example within 90 days after final submission unless MCAST policy requires otherwise).
  • A copy of the completed study may be provided to participants on request, as stated in the ethics form.

Risks and wellbeing

The activity is considered low risk: it uses a device camera for pose estimation in line with manufacturer guidance, in a safe environment, with the option to stand or sit and to take breaks. Some people may feel mild embarrassment related to seeing body posture visualised. You may pause or stop immediately without penalty. Outputs from the prototype are used strictly for this research and are not shared publicly or reused beyond the study without further explicit consent, as set out in the ethics documentation.

Session data tools (prototype)

The AR try-on page can export a session log (performance, optional pose coordinates, simple alignment proxies) and a separate post-task questionnaire as JSON files — still only for participants who have consented, and only when you choose to save and send them. Nothing is uploaded automatically by these static pages.
